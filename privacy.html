Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Z.O.U.R. Studio ("the app") is a personal Windows desktop app used by its owner. This policy explains what the app does with information from TikTok.

What the app reads

Only after you connect your TikTok account and allow it on TikTok's own page, the app reads, through TikTok's official API:

  • Your basic profile: your display name and TikTok's ID for your account.
  • Your account numbers: followers, following, total likes and number of videos.
  • Your videos' details: title or description, date, length, link, and their view, like, comment and share counts.

The app does not read your messages, your password, your followers' identities, or anything about other people.

How it's used

The app shows these numbers to you inside the app, and keeps a small daily record of your totals so it can show how they change over time.

Where it's kept

Everything stays on your own computer, in the app's folder. The sign-in token TikTok gives the app is stored encrypted with Windows' own data protection. The app has no server of its own: nothing is uploaded, sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising. The only place the app sends requests to is TikTok, to read the information above.

How to remove it

  • In the app: Team & trends › Accounts › TikTok › Disconnect. The app forgets the sign-in and asks TikTok to cancel it.
  • On TikTok: in Settings and privacy, open the list of apps you've given permission to, and remove Z.O.U.R. Studio.
  • Deleting the app's folder on your computer removes everything it kept.

Children

The app is not meant for children and is not offered to the public.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. Questions: message @zour.studio on TikTok.
